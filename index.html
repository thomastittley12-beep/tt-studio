Thomas Tittley Studio

Thomas Tittley Studio is a personal, self-hosted tool that Thomas Tittley uses to schedule and publish his own short videos to his own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram).

It is not a public service: only the owner's own accounts are connected, and no other users can sign up.

YouTube

Thomas Tittley Studio uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos to his own YouTube channel (@lab_content.ugc1) and to read their basic status. By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
