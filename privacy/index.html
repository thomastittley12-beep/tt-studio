Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Thomas Tittley Studio ("the App") is a personal, self-hosted tool used only by its owner, Thomas Tittley, to publish videos to his own accounts.

Data collected

When the owner connects one of his own accounts, the App stores the access tokens and basic profile information (account name, profile ID) returned by the platform, plus the videos and captions he uploads. No data is collected from other people.

Use of data

This data is used only to publish and schedule the owner's videos and to show their basic statistics to him. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is stored on the owner's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted.

Deletion

Disconnecting an account in the App deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (for example TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
